PRIVACY POLICY
Clima Mobile App
Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clima ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Clima mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1) DATA WE COLLECT

A. Precise and Approximate Location Data (with your permission)
- The App requests foreground location permission to detect your current location and provide local weather information.
- We may process latitude/longitude coordinates and reverse-geocoded city/country information.
- Location access is optional. If you do not grant location access, you can still use manual location search where available.

B. Search and Usage Inputs
- Country/city queries you enter manually to retrieve weather data.
- Basic in-app preferences (for example, display settings) may be stored on your device.

C. Device and Technical Data
- Standard technical information may be generated by your device/platform (for example, IP-based network details required for internet communication).
- We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal identifiers such as government IDs or biometric data.

2) HOW WE USE DATA

We use collected data to:
- Provide weather, air quality, and location-based features.
- Improve app reliability, usability, and performance.
- Troubleshoot and prevent abuse or misuse.
- Comply with legal obligations where applicable.

3) DATA SOURCES AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

To provide app features, the App sends relevant requests to third-party APIs/services, including:
- Weather data provider (WeatherAPI): to fetch current weather and air quality data.
- Geolocation/reverse geocoding services via Expo/OS capabilities.
- Country/city reference datasets (for manual location selection).

When you use these features, limited data (such as location coordinates or location query text) may be transmitted to these services as necessary to return results.

Please review third-party privacy practices directly:
- WeatherAPI Privacy Policy: https://www.weatherapi.com/privacy.aspx
- Expo Privacy Policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

4) DATA SHARING

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share limited data only in these cases:
- Service Providers: with API/infrastructure providers strictly to operate app features.
- Legal Compliance: if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
- Protection: to protect rights, safety, and integrity of users, the App, or the public.

5) DATA RETENTION

- We retain personal data only for as long as needed to provide the App and fulfill legitimate business or legal purposes.
- Most weather/location requests are processed in real time.
- Local preferences may remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App.

6) DATA SECURITY

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7) YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data.

You can also:
- Revoke location access at any time in device settings.
- Stop data collection by uninstalling the App.

For data requests, contact us using the details in Section 11.

8) CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9) INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own by service providers that support the App. Where required, we take appropriate safeguards for cross-border data transfers.

10) CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version in the App/repository and update the "Last updated" date.
